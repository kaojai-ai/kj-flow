# Community code of conduct

KJ Flow welcomes contributors of every background and experience level.

Be respectful, constructive, and specific. Assume good intent, discuss ideas
rather than people, and make space for others to participate. Harassment,
discrimination, threats, doxxing, and deliberate disruption are not accepted.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
behavior harms the community. Report conduct concerns privately through the
contact options at [kaojai.ai](https://kaojai.ai).
